The founding principle

Opportunity is temporary. Regret is optional.

Inspired by a Canadian soldier's personal deployment experience, Pre-Jerk Theory examines an ordinary human need under extraordinary scheduling constraints. The premise: never assume free time will be available when you want it.

The official manifesto

Time is uncertain. Opportunity is finite. Relief is precious.

We live in a world of unpredictable schedules, extended obligations, and responsibilities that rarely ask permission before taking over our lives.

Pre-Jerk Theory emerged from the experience of a Canadian soldier who learned that a free moment today does not guarantee a free moment tomorrow.

Its proposition is simple: recognize opportunity, understand your circumstances, and do not take the availability of personal time for granted.

The premise is humorous. The underlying principle is universal.

Preemptively, empty the balls.

End of doctrine. Proceed with discretion.
